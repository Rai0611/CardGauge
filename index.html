A NOTE ON THE STANDARDS

Centering is not a grade guarantee.

Targets are simplified centering references for PSA 10, BGS Pristine 10, CGC Gem Mint 10, and SGC 10. The SGC target is an estimate. TAG is included for ratio measurements, but no TAG-specific numeric cutoff is configured because we could not verify one in its published information. This checker does not model a complete grade; confirm current criteria with the grader.
